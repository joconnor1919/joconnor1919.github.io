All About Cheddar Cheese

What Is Cheddar Cheese?

Cheddar is a popular cheese made from cow's milk. It can taste mild and creamy or sharp and rich, depending on how long it ages.

Where Does Cheddar Come From?

Cheddar cheese takes its name from the village of Cheddar in Somerset, England. Today, cheddar is made in many countries.

Types of Cheddar

Mild cheddar: Creamy and gentle in flavor.

Sharp cheddar: Stronger in flavor after aging longer.

White cheddar: Cheddar without added orange coloring.

Fun Fact

The orange color in some cheddar comes from annatto, a natural coloring. It does not necessarily mean the cheese has aged longer.
